A public-interest example operated by Quintessential Digital:

DentistRadar

Public-interest platform

DentistRadar is a free tool that helps people find NHS dentists by alerting users when availability changes.

It was designed, built, and is operated by Quintessential Digital as a real-world example of how calm, well-governed digital systems can reduce friction and improve access where existing processes fall short.

Independent and not affiliated with the NHS.
